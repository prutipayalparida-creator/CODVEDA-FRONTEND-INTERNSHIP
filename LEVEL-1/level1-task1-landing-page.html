Three ideas, one timer

Everything in Solstice exists to protect one long, uninterrupted stretch of work each day.

01 — Adaptive pacing

Sessions that bend to your energy

Block length compresses in the early morning and evening, and stretches near your personal peak hours.

Session transitions are shown with light and color instead of sound — nothing to break your concentration.

03 — Daily arc
